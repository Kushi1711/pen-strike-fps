HOW TO PLAY

✓ Setup

Position your webcam to see your hand and pen clearly. Make sure you have adequate lighting.

✓ Aiming

Hold a pen in your hand and move it in front of the camera. The crosshair will follow your pen's position. Move it to aim at enemies.

✓ Shooting

To fire, make a quick forward thrust motion with your pen toward the camera. This gesture triggers the shot.

✓ Combat

Eliminate all enemies before they defeat you. Your health is shown top-left. Kill count and ammunition shown top-right and bottom-right.

✓ Waves

Complete waves of enemies with increasing difficulty. Each kill adds to your score.

✓ Health & Ammo

Manage your ammunition carefully. Reloading happens automatically when you run out. Heal by completing waves (health regenerates).

✓ Tips

• Keep steady aim for accurate shots
• Quick, decisive shooting gestures work best
• Take cover behind environmental objects
• Stay aware of multiple enemies
• Listen for audio cues
